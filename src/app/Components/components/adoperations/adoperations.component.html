AD Operation

We Manages Your ad Trafficking campaign optimizations, media planning and reporting to deliver results with an acute focus on quality and efficiency.

Digital Ad Operations with Admediaworld (also known as ad operations, ad ops, online ad operations) denotes to the process that support the managing and delivering the advertisements through digital platforms. The Digital Ad Operations includes display (banner and rich media ads), video ads, text ads, search ads, mobile advertising and many more.

Ad Operations is a team associated with our publishers Accounts to maximize their revenue by serving the relevant advertisements on their web pages /Mobile apps or OTT platforms. Ad Ops is responsible to set up and optimize the ad campaigns by following the various checks and policies. Also, Ad Ops usually interacts with the various platforms including ad exchanges, ad networks, ad servers, SSPs, DMPs, and more.

The Ad Ops team needs to follow certain roles and responsibilities no matter they handle one advertiser and multiple advertisers on their day-to-day operations. A few major responsibilities listed here are:

  • Trafficking: It involves uploading the creative assets provided by the advertiser in to the ad servers and update the necessary settings to ensure that the ad campaign will meet the advertiser goals. The Ad Ops team reviews the media plans/ insertion orders, check the inventory and upload the creative to the ad servers.
  • Scheduling: Scheduling the campaigns are also plays a vital role in success of ad campaigns. Thus, Ad Ops team and advertiser will decide when to serve the ads to reach their targeted audience and settings will be updated accordingly. Scheduling the ads including the day when to serve and time to serve the ads acquire the desired audience during a specific time or date range.
  • Optimization: Optimizing the ad campaigns is another crucial task to achieve the campaign goals during the campaign run dates. There are many factors influences the ad servers to publish the advertisements on a website which may impact the campaign goals. Due to this reason, Ad Ops team always review the performance of the ad campaigns and update the relevant settings to meet the agreed goals.
  • Troubleshoot ad serving issues: Working with multiple advertisers/ agencies/ networks along with complexities of the requirements and creative assets provided may turns into technical issues. It may cause ads not serving, breaking your web pages, slowdown the web pages, forced ad redirects, dropping viewability, and other issues. However, Ad Ops team keeps checking these issues and fix them at the earliest to avoid any impact.
  • Managing the Reports: Generating and analysing the ad campaign performance report from the first party and third-party ad services, and demand partners will be handled by the Ad Ops team. The ad campaign reports usually consist of discrepancies, impression level metrics, and attribution metrics.
  • Demand Management: Most of the premium publishers have their own sales teams to manage their ads demand. However, these activities are automated and Ad Ops can check the available inventory using the ad servers and work with the sales teams to work on accordingly to optimally utilize the ad inventory.
  • Yield Management: What Ad Ops team does, boils down to one thing - Revenue. The Ad Ops team/ team member taking care of yield management and look for various opportunities to maximizing the revenue through advertising to the publisher. For ex: working with the website development team to creating high performing ad slots on the webpages, increasing the user interactions through improved advertising experience, analysing the pricing for different ad slots and work with the finance team to finalizing the rate cards.

These are glimpse of Ad Operations team does, however, there are multiple responsibilities that team handles which depends on the publisher requirements. The bottom line to have this team setup is to take care of ad serving activities to maximize the publisher's revenue.
